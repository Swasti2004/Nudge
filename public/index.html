The useful details

Questions before you get started?

A few straight answers about what Nudge can—and can't—do.

What is Nudge?

Nudge is an AI-powered assistant that turns opportunities, deadlines, and follow-ups into organized items you can act on.

What can I capture?

You can add supported links, posters, screenshots, flyers, or a quick written description of the opportunity.

How does Nudge identify deadlines?

Nudge extracts dates from what you share and asks you to confirm them before they are saved.

Is Nudge only for internships?

No. It also works for jobs, hackathons, scholarships, events, talks, and any opportunity with a date attached.

How is Nudge different from a job portal?

A job portal helps you discover listings. Nudge helps you keep track of and follow through on the ones you find, wherever they came from.

Will Nudge automatically apply for me?

No. Nudge organizes the details and reminds you what to do next. You decide when and how to apply.

Are reminders supported?

Nudge keeps upcoming deadlines, tasks, and events in view so nothing slips by. Reminder options depend on your settings.
